Where do you offer pickup and local delivery? +

We are a private small-batch bakehouse based in Ruskin, Florida, with convenient Ruskin pickup. We also arrange delivery across SouthShore and eastern Hillsborough County—including Brandon, Valrico, Plant City, Seffner, Wimauma, Temple Terrace, Thonotosassa, Apollo Beach, and Sun City Center.

How do ordering, approval, and Square invoicing work? +

Submit your flavors, quantities, and requested date via our online Jotform. Standard retail orders pay securely through Square. Custom cakes, logo cookies, and corporate orders are personally reviewed for availability and artwork details before approval and Square invoicing.

What are the minimums for Stuffed Cookies and Logo Cookies? +

Signature Petite Cookies are sold by the dozen ($18). Molten-Center Stuffed Cookies are $4 each with a minimum of 6. Custom printed Logo Cookies are $5 each with a minimum of 12. Corporate Sampler Boxes require a 10-box minimum.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary requests? +

Please contact us before placing your order if you have a specific food allergy. Because our kitchen works with wheat, dairy, eggs, peanuts, and tree nuts, we want to review your needs directly before confirming.

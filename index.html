05 / Questions

FAQ

Where are you located?

Home is Cookie is a private bakehouse in Ruskin, Florida, with local pickup and regional delivery throughout the Tampa Bay area.

Do you offer custom corporate gifting?

Yes. We create custom branded cookies, company logo cookies, gifting boxes, and event treats tailored to your business or celebration.

Are orders baked fresh?

Absolutely. Every batch is mixed and baked to order, never frozen, and prepared around your order window.

Can I order for an event?

Yes. We regularly create tasting trays, cookie cakes, and custom gift boxes for parties, events, team gifts, and client appreciation.
